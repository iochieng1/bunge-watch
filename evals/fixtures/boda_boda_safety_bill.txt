THE BODA BODA SAFETY BILL, 2099

A Bill for AN ACT of Parliament to provide for the registration of motorcycle taxi operators, rider training and
passenger safety, and for connected purposes.

ENACTED by the Parliament of Kenya, as follows-

PART I - PRELIMINARY

1. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Boda Boda Safety Act, 2099.

2. Interpretation
In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires-
"county registrar" means the officer appointed by a county government under section 4;
"rider" means a person who carries a passenger on a motorcycle for hire or reward.

PART II - REGISTRATION AND TRAINING

3. Registration of riders
(1) A person shall not operate a motorcycle for hire unless registered with the county registrar.
(2) The county registrar shall issue each registered rider with a reflective jacket bearing a registration
number.

4. Training
(1) Every rider shall complete a road safety training course of not less than forty hours before registration.
(2) The training course shall be offered free of charge at every county vocational training centre.

PART III - PASSENGER SAFETY AND OFFENCES

5. Helmets
(1) A rider shall provide every passenger with a helmet that meets the standard set by the Kenya Bureau of
Standards.
(2) A rider shall not carry more than one passenger at a time.

6. Offences and penalties
(1) A rider who contravenes section 5 commits an offence and is liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding
ten thousand shillings.
(2) A person who operates a motorcycle for hire without registration commits an offence and is liable to a fine
not exceeding twenty thousand shillings or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding three months, or to both.

FIRST SCHEDULE
CONTENTS OF THE TRAINING COURSE

1. Traffic rules and road signs.
2. First aid for road accident victims.
3. Defensive riding in wet weather.
