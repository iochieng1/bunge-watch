THE COUNTY WATER SERVICES (LEVY) BILL, 2099

A Bill for AN ACT of Parliament to establish a water conservation levy, provide for exemptions and create the
Water Conservation Fund.

Clause 1. Short title and commencement
This Act may be cited as the County Water Services (Levy) Act, 2099 and shall come into operation on the first
day of July, 2099.

Clause 2. Water conservation levy
(1) There shall be charged a water conservation levy of two per cent of the amount billed by every licensed water
service provider to a commercial customer.
(2) The levy shall be collected by the water service provider and remitted to the county treasury by the
twentieth day of the following month.

Clause 3. Exemptions
The levy shall not be charged on water supplied to households, public schools, public hospitals or places of
worship.

Clause 4. Water Conservation Fund
(1) There is established a Water Conservation Fund into which all money collected under clause 2 shall be paid.
(2) The Fund shall be used to protect water catchment areas, repair leaking pipes and drill boreholes in
arid areas.

Clause 5. Late remittance
A water service provider who fails to remit the levy on time shall pay a penalty of five per cent of the unpaid
amount for each month or part of a month that it remains unpaid.

Clause 6. Annual report
The county executive committee member responsible for water shall, within three months after the end of each
financial year, submit a report on the Fund to the county assembly.
