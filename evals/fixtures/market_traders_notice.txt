Memorandum on the proposed Open Air Markets (Traders' Welfare) Bill, 2099. The proposal would require every
county government to provide clean toilets, running water and lighting in each open air market that has more
than one hundred registered traders. Market committees elected by the traders themselves would manage the
facilities and report quarterly to the county department responsible for trade.

The proposal also caps the daily market fee that a county may charge a trader at fifty shillings, and requires
that the fee be paid through a mobile money paybill with a receipt sent to the trader. Counties that charge more
than the cap would have to refund the excess within thirty days.

Traders who sell perishable food would be entitled to a shaded stall with drainage. Hawkers who are not
registered would be given ninety days to register before any enforcement action is taken against them.
